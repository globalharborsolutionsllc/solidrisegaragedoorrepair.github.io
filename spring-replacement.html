Warning Signs

Signs Your Garage Door Spring Is Broken

A broken spring is a safety hazard. If you notice any of these signs, call us immediately — never try to operate a door with a broken spring.

Door Won't Open

If your garage door suddenly won't open or feels extremely heavy, a broken torsion spring is almost always the cause.

Loud Bang or Snap

A loud bang from your garage — like a gunshot — is the classic sound of a torsion spring breaking under tension.

Door Opens Crooked

If one side rises faster than the other, an extension spring may have broken on one side, causing uneven movement.

Visible Gap in Spring

A visible 2–3 inch gap in your torsion spring is a clear sign it has snapped and needs immediate replacement.

Opener Runs But Door Doesn't Move

Your opener motor runs but the door won't lift — the spring has lost tension and the motor can't carry the full weight alone.

Spring Looks Stretched or Worn

Springs have a limited cycle life. If your spring looks stretched, corroded, or uneven, it's close to breaking — replace it before it snaps.
