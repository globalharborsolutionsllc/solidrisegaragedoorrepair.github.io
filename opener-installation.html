Opener Types

Which Garage Door Opener Is Right for You?

Our technicians will help you choose the best opener type based on your garage size, ceiling height, and noise preferences.

Belt Drive

The quietest option available — ideal for garages attached to living spaces or bedrooms. Smooth, vibration-free operation. Slightly higher cost but worth it for noise-sensitive homes.

✓ Best for attached garages

Chain Drive

The most affordable and durable opener type. Slightly louder than belt drive but extremely reliable and perfect for detached garages where noise isn't a concern.

✓ Best value option

Smart Wi-Fi Opener

Control your garage from your phone with myQ or similar apps. Get open/close alerts, let in guests remotely, and integrate with your smart home system. Our fastest-growing install type.

✓ Best for tech-forward homes

Jackshaft / Wall-Mount

Mounts to the wall beside the door instead of the ceiling — perfect for garages with high or cathedral ceilings, limited headroom, or where ceiling space is needed for storage.

✓ Best for high ceilings
Our Process

How We Install Your New Opener

A professional installation takes 1.5 to 3 hours from start to finish. We handle everything — you just press the button.

1

Assess Your Garage

We inspect ceiling height, door weight, existing wiring, and bracket positioning to determine the best opener model and mounting configuration.

2

Mount the Rail & Motor

We securely mount the rail system and motor unit to the ceiling, ensuring proper alignment with your door's center bracket.

3

Connect & Wire

We connect the opener to your wall button, safety sensors, and power source — and set up Wi-Fi if it's a smart opener model.

4

Program & Test Everything

We program all remotes, keypads, and your phone app — then test open/close cycles, force limits, and safety reverse before we leave.
